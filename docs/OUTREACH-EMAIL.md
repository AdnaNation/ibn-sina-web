Subject: Request for a short discussion — independent hospital digital-experience prototype

Dear Team,

I am a web developer based in Bangladesh, and I have independently built a demonstration prototype exploring how a hospital website, appointment journey, and live patient-queue experience could work together.

The prototype includes a bilingual patient-facing website, doctor and branch directories, appointment and queue-tracking flows, and a role-based staff workspace. It is a concept demonstration only; it is not an official Ibn Sina Trust system, is not commissioned or endorsed by the Trust, and is not intended for real patient data or operational use.

I would appreciate the opportunity to give the relevant team a short walkthrough and hear whether any of the ideas align with your digital-service priorities. I would also welcome feedback on the workflows, security expectations, and integration requirements that would need to be considered before any formal project could be discussed.

If appropriate, could you please direct me to the person or department responsible for digital services, IT, or patient-experience initiatives?

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,

Azizul Islam Adnan
Web Developer
